You know AI is powerful. Your week is already full.

You have seen what AI can do. But your job, your family and your calendar leave little room to figure out how to turn that into something real. So the idea stays in a drawer: a side business, a skill that is yours, another source of income.

Meanwhile, plenty of local cafes, tradespeople and shops still run on an old website, or none at all. Most of them are not waiting for a large agency. They are waiting for someone to ask.

A gold rush, and a shortage of shovels.

The tools exist. Until recently, a good website took a small team: a designer, a developer, a writer, a manager. For simple sites, that can now be one person directing an AI. What has been missing is a clear path for a beginner. That is what this course is.

How it works

You do the thinking. Claude does the typing. Your guide keeps you moving.

  1. 1. Learn the few words that matter

    You will not learn to code. You learn the plain-English words to tell Claude what you want, and how to check what it made.

  2. 2. Build real sites with Claude

    Claude writes the code and puts your site online. You steer, preview it, and ask for changes until it is right.

  3. 3. Reach the businesses that need you

    Pick a niche, find local businesses, and get help with outreach, pricing, proposals and follow-up.

Your first 30 days

The pace the course is designed around: about one hour a day for 30 days.

  1. Days 1 to 3

    Set up and learn to direct Claude

    Install your tools and connect them. Learn how to ask Claude for what you want in plain English.

  2. Days 4 to 7

    Build your first site

    Make a first section of your own portfolio with Claude, choose a look, and put your first site live.

  3. Weeks 2 and 3

    Start talking to businesses

    Choose a niche, build a list of local businesses, and begin reaching out. This is the step that leads to a first client.

  4. By day 30

    The foundations of an agency

    A live portfolio, a way to price and propose work, a client tracker, and outreach under way. A base to keep building on.

This is the plan, not a promise. Your pace will vary, and nothing guarantees a client or an income. What the plan gives you is a clear next step every day.

Questions

Do I need to know how to code?

No. Claude writes the code. You learn to describe what you want, check the result, and ask for changes.

Is one hour a day really enough?

It is the pace the first 30 days are built around. Setting up your tools can take more than one sitting, so you spread it over a few days. Your speed will vary.

Will I get clients?

Nobody can honestly promise that, and this course does not. What it gives you is a system: pick a niche, find local businesses, reach out, follow up, and track every attempt. It measures what you control, which is how many conversations you start.

What does it cost?

The checkout page shows the current price of the course. Claude, Eden and GitHub each have their own plans too. Check their pages for current prices.

I use Claude, but never to build things. Is this for me?

Yes. If you are comfortable chatting with Claude, you already have the first skill. The course adds the building and the business.
